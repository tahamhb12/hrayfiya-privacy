# Delete Your Hrayfiya Account

To delete your account and associated data, you have two options:

## Option 1 — From inside the app (recommended)

1. Open Hrayfiya
2. Go to **Profile** → **Support**
3. Send a message: "I want to delete my account"
4. Our team will process the request

## Option 2 — By email (if you no longer have the app)

Send an email to **tahamhb12@gmail.com** with:
- Subject: "Account Deletion Request"
- Your registered email address
- Your full name

## What gets deleted

- Your profile (name, email, phone, photo, addresses)
- Your bookings history
- Your chat messages
- Your ratings and favorites

## What is retained

Anonymized booking records for legal/accounting purposes (no
personal identifiers), retained for up to 5 years as required
by local law.

## Processing time

Your request is processed within **30 days**.
