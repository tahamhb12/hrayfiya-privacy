# Privacy Policy for Hrayfiya

Last updated: May 7, 2026

Hrayfiya ("we", "our", "us") operates the Hrayfiya mobile application
(the "App"), a service marketplace connecting customers with service
providers.

## Information We Collect

- **Account information**: email address, name, phone number, profile photo
- **Location data**: precise location (with your permission) to find
  nearby service providers
- **Camera and photos**: to upload profile pictures, shop pictures, and
  scan QR codes (only when you choose to use these features)
- **Storage access**: to select and upload images from your device
- **Messages**: chat messages exchanged between customers and service
  providers
- **Bookings and payments**: service bookings, ratings, and transaction
  history
- **Device information**: push notification tokens for sending booking
  and chat notifications

## How We Use Your Information

- Provide and maintain the service (matching customers and workers)
- Process bookings, payments, and ratings
- Enable in-app chat between users
- Send booking confirmations and notifications
- Improve our App and customer support

## Data Storage and Security

Data is stored securely on Supabase infrastructure with encryption in
transit (HTTPS) and access controls (Row Level Security).

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information is shared only:
- Between customers and the service providers they book
- With service providers required to operate the App (Supabase, Google
  Maps, Expo Notifications)
- When required by law

## Permissions Used

- **CAMERA**: to take profile/shop photos and scan QR codes
- **READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE**: to upload images from your gallery
- **ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION**: to find nearby
  service providers

All permissions require your explicit consent.

## Your Rights

You can:
- Access, edit, or delete your account from the app
- Request data deletion by contacting us
- Disable permissions in your device settings

## Children's Privacy

Hrayfiya is not intended for users under 13.
